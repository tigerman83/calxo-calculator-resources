Definitions

The conversion definitions are exact. JavaScript uses binary floating-point arithmetic; the digits shown do not establish the precision of a measured speed. Reverse uses the calculation value, rather than the rounded display.

Average speed is not peak speed

100 yards in 12.5 seconds is an average of 8 yd/s, or approximately 16.36 mph. It does not describe the runner's speed at every moment.
